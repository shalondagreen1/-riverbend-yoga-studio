About Our Studio

Meet Our Instructors

Our instructors focus on patient guidance, clear instructions, and helping students find comfortable movement. Individual instructor names and biographies will be confirmed by the studio.

Accessibility Accommodations

Students are encouraged to ask about chair-based options, modifications, accessible studio entry, and other accommodations before visiting. We aim to make participation comfortable for a wide range of needs.

Studio Policies

Arrive a few minutes early, respect other participants, and let your instructor know about any movement concerns. Wear comfortable clothing and work within your own limits. Contact the studio to confirm cancellation and booking policies.
